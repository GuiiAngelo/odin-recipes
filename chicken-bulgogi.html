Skillet Chicken Bulgogi

A delicious Korean dish made with tender chicken strips cooked in a savory sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 1/4 cup chopped onion
  • 5 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon cayenne
  • salt and ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 pound skinless, boneless chicken breasts, cut into thin strips

Step by step:

  1. Mix the chopped onion, soy sauce, brown sugar, minced garlic, sesame oil, sesame seeds, and cayenne in a bowl.
  2. Season the mixture with salt and black pepper.
  3. Add the chicken strips and mix until they are evenly coated.
  4. Cover and marinate the chicken for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Heat a pan over medium-high heat.
  6. Cook the chicken until it is fully cooked and lightly browned.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy.

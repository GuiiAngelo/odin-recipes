Macaroni and Cheese

A classic comfort food dish made with macaroni and a creamy cheese sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 1 box elbow macaroni
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • ground black pepper to taste
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese

Step by step:

  1. Cook the elbow macaroni according to the package instructions.
  2. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat.
  3. Stir in the flour, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Gradually add the milk while stirring until the mixture becomes smooth and thick.
  5. Add the shredded Cheddar cheese and stir until melted.
  6. Add the cooked macaroni to the cheese sauce and mix well.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy.

Pizza

A delicious margherita pizza made with fresh ingredients.

Ingredients:

  • Wheat flour
  • Water
  • Baking powder
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Basil leaves

Step by step:

  1. Mix the wheat flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add water and olive oil, then mix until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough for a few minutes until it becomes smooth.
  4. Roll the dough into a thin pizza base.
  5. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  6. Add the mozzarella cheese on top.
  7. Bake the pizza until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  8. Remove the pizza from the oven and add the basil leaves.
  9. Slice and serve the pizza.
